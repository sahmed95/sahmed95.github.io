About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Economics at the University of Washington, expecting to finish in fall 2026. My work combines microeconomic theory — particularly contract theory — with structural estimation methods applied to high-dimensional demand settings.

I spent a summer as an Economist Intern at Amazon, where I built structural demand models and counterfactual simulation tools. Alongside my research, I teach Game Theory as an independent instructor and serve as a TA for Graduate Microeconomic Theory at UW.
